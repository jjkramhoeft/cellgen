# Resource Overview: Generic Resources and Their Variants

*Compiled 2026-11-12 from [game-resources-and-trade.md](research/game-resources-and-trade.md) §5.2–5.3 (the 39 generic game resources) and [patch-resources.md](research/patch-resources.md) §4–5 (the ~370 catalogue variants). Row numbers (`#`, `F`) refer to that catalogue. This is a decision aid: the first column is the generic resource, the second lists every variant the docs put under it.*

*Added on 2026-11-12, here and in `game-resources-and-trade.md` §5.2–5.3: **Crystal** (raw, mined) and **Glass** (processed from it). With them the set is 39 resources: 24 raw, 9 intermediate, 6 finished.*

## Processed resources (tiers 2 and 3)

These have no catalogue variants of their own; the second column shows the inputs or forms the docs give.

| Main resource | Variants / inputs |
|---|---|
| **Charcoal** | From timber; mangrove charcoal |
| **Iron** | Iron ore + fuel (charcoal) |
| **Bronze** | Copper ore + tin + fuel |
| **Cloth** | From fibre (linen, hemp, cotton, silk) or wool |
| **Leather** | Livestock hides + tannin: oak bark and galls, sumac, mangrove bark, acacia pods and bark, chestnut and hemlock bark (#241–245) |
| **Bricks & lime** | Clay or limestone + fuel |
| **Glass** — *added* | Crystal + fuel, with a flux: plant ash and potash (#320) or natron and soda (#171). Window glass, bottles, vessels, beads |
| **Preserved food** | Fish or livestock + salt (salt fish, salt meat, cheese) |
| **Naval stores** | Pine, larch, fir; resin, tar and pitch |
| **Tools** | Iron + timber |
| **Arms & armour** | Iron or bronze + timber + leather |
| **Clothing** | Cloth + leather |
| **Drink & oil** | Ale (grain), wine and olive oil (orchard) |
| **Fine goods** | Gold or silver work, gems, dyed cloth and silk, porcelain (kaolin) |
| **Coin** (optional) | Silver or gold |

## Variants mapped twice

The mapping table puts three variants under two generic resources each:

- Oilseeds (#35–39): Produce and Orchard. Listed under Orchard above.
- Murex (#161): Gems & pearls and Dyes. Listed under both above.
- Malachite and azurite (#365): Copper ore and Gems & pearls. Listed under Copper ore above.

One catalogue row is split by the Crystal addition: #371 (quartz crystal, amethyst, agate). Quartz crystal goes to Crystal; amethyst and agate stay under Gems & pearls.
