cellgen · Sandbox 3 · Map hierarchy

An unbounded base map in metres, built from three levels of Voronoi cells. Every cell is a pure function of the seed and the parameters, so the world has no edge: pan anywhere and the same seed always gives the same map.

The three levels

  • Regions (128 km, straight borders) own the biomes whose site lies inside them. Each region picks 0–2 ocean seeds and 2–4 biome merges among its biomes.
  • Biomes (32 km, warped borders) are the land/ocean unit. Each biome picks 2–4 subbiome merges among the subbiomes it owns. Colours are cosmetic.
  • Subbiomes (8 km, warped borders) carry detail: sub-biomes, towns, ore, factions.

Oceans

  • Seeds: each region turns 0–2 of its biomes into ocean.
  • Links: with a 40 % chance a seed links to its nearest other seed; every biome on the straight line between them becomes ocean, so several seeds can join into one ocean.
  • Growth: every linked ocean biome converts its 3 / 2 / 1 / 0 closest land neighbours with chances 10 / 33 / 33 / 23 %.
  • Colour by Ocean genesis and turn on Ocean seeds & links and Growth arrows to see each step.

Navigation

  • Scroll to zoom at the cursor, drag to pan, pinch on touch, double-click zooms in (Shift zooms out).
  • Click pins the biome (yellow outline) and subbiome (cyan outline) under the cursor; Esc releases.
  • Shift+drag or M measures a distance, with travel times for a horse, a marching legion and a hiker.
  • + / − zoom · 0 / H origin · arrows pan · R new seed · 1–8 or [ ] colour mode · B biome borders · N subbiome borders · L links · W growth · J merges · S sites · G grid · ? help.

Arrows connect cell sites. With border warp a site can sit slightly outside its own warped cell. Travel speeds are reference data from sandbox3.md; the generator does not use them.
