cellgen · Sandbox 2 · Voronoi cells

Voronoi cells from a jittered lattice: one site per lattice cell, at a hashed offset. The diagram is a pure function of position, so it is exact at every zoom level and has no edge. Up to four octaves of cells are layered into a fractal. A threshold on the per-cell value splits land from water at the ratio you choose.

Navigation

  • Scroll to zoom at the cursor, drag to pan, pinch on touch. Use the minimap to jump.
  • Click a cell to pin it (selected octave). Esc releases it. Double-click zooms in (Shift zooms out).
  • + / − zoom, 0 or F fit, arrow keys pan.
  • R new seed · M / Shift+M next / previous metric · [ ] colour mode · 1–4 octaves · E edges · S sites · G lattice grid · P probe · ? help.

Reading the map

  • Distance probe: the yellow ball has radius F1 and touches the nearest site; the teal one has radius F2 and touches the second-nearest. Their shape is the metric's unit ball.
  • Land & water: every cell gets a value from smooth noise at its site, plus per-cell randomness and an optional island shape. The threshold is the quantile that gives the target land share, so coastlines run along Voronoi edges at every octave.
  • Statistics: islands, lakes, coastline length and its box-counting dimension. More octaves and a higher gain push the dimension up.
