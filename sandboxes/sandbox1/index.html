cellgen · Sandbox 1

Generate cells, configure the methods and their parameters on the left, and evaluate the result visually on the right. Every stage runs on whichever cell structure you choose.

Navigation

  • Scroll to zoom at the cursor, drag to pan, pinch on touch.
  • Click a cell to pin it (neighbours highlighted). Esc to unpin.
  • Double-click zooms in; Shift + double-click zooms out.
  • + / − zoom, 0 or F fit, arrow keys pan, R random seed, E toggle edges, [ ] previous/next field.
  • Zoom far enough and every cell shows its value.

What's inside

  • Cells: Voronoi meshes (Delaunator, circumcenter or centroid corners, Lloyd relaxation), square/hex/triangle grids, and distance-function cells with L1/L2/L∞/Lp metrics, weighted Voronoi variants and domain-warped borders.
  • Sampling: Poisson disc (Bridson, variable density), jittered lattices, best-candidate, uniform, sunflower spiral.
  • Noise: seeded hash-based simplex, fBm, ridged, Quilez domain warping, SDF island masks.
  • World: template blobs, tectonic plates, flux erosion, Priority-Flood hydrology, wind/rain sweep, Whittaker biomes.
  • Networks: space colonization rivers (Runions et al.) and noise-isoline networks with gradient-normalised width.
  • Cellular automata: the 4-5 cave rule generalised to irregular graphs.
  • Determinism: integer hashing and deterministic transcendental functions; the fingerprint is identical in every browser.

Research notes: docs/research/ in the repository.
